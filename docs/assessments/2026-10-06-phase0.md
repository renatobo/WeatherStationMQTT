## Broker-to-storage observation

Mosquitto, sensorslog and MariaDB were active on raspi5. A unique passive MQTT subscriber observed only office/workshop topics for 370 seconds; no telemetry was published by the observer. The first/last received events were 10:27:33 and 10:33:35 PDT.

- Workshop: 2 temperature messages, 2 humidity messages, 37 presence messages. Metric pairs were approximately 301 seconds apart.
- Office: 2 temperature messages and 2 humidity messages, approximately 301 seconds apart.
- All 8 metric messages matched MariaDB by payload timestamp, source, unit and numeric value. This checks actual ingestion rather than trusting publish return values. Presence is outside the metric storage contract.
- Broker log excerpt for 00:03:10–10:33:10 PDT: office 130 new connections / 129 keepalive timeouts; workshop 75 new connections / 60 timeouts / 6 duplicate-client-ID closures. These are counts within the captured log coverage, not whole-day publication counts or proof of duplicate physical devices.
- Ten ICMP samples per device: 0% loss; average latency workshop 43.760ms and office 41.204ms. Both `/info` and `/temp` returned HTTP 200. Short observations cannot exclude intermittent RF/power issues.

See [machine-readable comparison](2026-10-06-phase0-telemetry.json). Raw MQTT payloads, database output, chip identity and broker addresses are kept in `.recovery/2026-10-06-phase0-observations/`.

## Limits and Phase 1 handoff

Phase 0 establishes pinned compilation, rollback artifacts, workshop image identity and a measured telemetry baseline. It does not repair the 17 assessed issues. Compiler warnings still identify timestamp/time-format overflow or truncation risks and simpleDSTadjust operator precedence. CodeRabbit is not installed; verification uses compiler output, source review, independent dependency comparison and live read-back.

HTTP still exposes unauthenticated management information. Reset reason is the last boot cause, not a historical reset counter; RSSI is a current sample, not an RF survey. Office legacy identity remains unknown until its firmware is explicitly updated. No eero settings, collector logic or broker settings changed. Long soak, outage tests, physical recovery rehearsal and office rollout belong to later phases.
